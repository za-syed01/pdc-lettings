What We Look For

We run a standard referencing process to keep our properties well looked after. Here's exactly what you'll need when you apply — no surprises.

01

Proof of Income

Combined income of around 2× the annual rent. Recent payslips or 3 months of bank statements.

02

Credit History

A standard credit check is run. No CCJs or significant adverse credit in the last three years.

03

References

A landlord or employer reference. First-time renters — just tell us and we'll work through it with you.

04

Right to Rent

All adult occupants must provide UK Right to Rent proof. A valid passport or visa document is fine.
